How big is one video

irisAI saves the camera's own stream without re-encoding, so quality is exactly what the camera produces. Measured across our Full HD recordings, a packing video averages 15 MB per minute, so a typical 2.5 minute pack is about 38 MB. A packing bench barely moves, which is why the files are smaller than a film of the same length. 720p roughly halves that; 4K is about four times larger.

Which one suits you

Cloud storage

  • Watch any video from anywhere, on any device
  • Nothing to buy or look after in the warehouse
  • Safe from theft, fire or a failed disk on site
  • Needs a steady internet line all day
  • The bill never ends and grows with every camera
  • Customer videos leave your premises

Local drive

  • Pay once. A second camera costs nothing extra
  • Works with no internet. Playback is instant
  • Videos never leave the building
  • Watching from outside needs remote access set up
  • A single drive can fail. A mirrored NAS keeps two copies
  • A drive needs replacing every few years
Our recommendation

At a few hundred videos a day on an archive plan, cloud is the cheaper and simpler start, and there is nothing to look after on site. A local drive wins once you keep videos longer, add cameras, or pass roughly a terabyte in storage, because it is paid for once. irisAI works with either, and many customers do both: everything saved locally, with only disputed videos sent to the cloud.

How the numbers are worked out
  • Video size: measured from real irisAI recordings. Full HD averages 15 MB per minute (range 6 to 26 depending on movement and the camera's bitrate setting); 4K about 60 MB per minute; 720p estimated at 8 MB per minute. Videos are stored as the camera sends them, with no re-encoding.
  • Cloud: storage price per GB per month as selected (AWS Glacier Instant Retrieval ₹0.42, Wasabi or Backblaze B2 about ₹0.60, AWS S3 Infrequent Access ₹1.15, AWS S3 Standard ₹2.10, all Mumbai region list prices). An internet line at ₹1,000 a month is added only if selected. On top of storage, a 30% cushion covers upload and playback fees, 18% GST and rupee-dollar movement, so the real bill should land under this page rather than over it. Archive plans charge every video for at least 90 days, which the calculator applies.
  • Local NAS: Synology DS224+ 2-bay unit about ₹34,000, plus two drives sized to your keep period (4 TB ₹10,000, 8 TB ₹18,000, 12 TB ₹27,000, 16 TB ₹36,000 each), mirrored so every video is kept twice, plus 18% GST. Electricity about ₹1,000 a year.
  • Local single drive: one drive of the same size fitted in the irisAI station PC. No second copy.
  • Indicative Indian retail and provider list prices, October 2026, rounded. Your exact bill will differ; the comparison will not.
